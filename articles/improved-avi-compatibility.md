I’ve updated MConverter’s AVI output to make converted videos more compatible with Windows Media Player and other older players.

Previously, AVI videos produced by MConverter could sometimes display a black screen when viewed in Windows Media Player. Similarly, on older devices, the audio could be missing from playback. This new update resolves these issues by improving the encoding process and resulting in improved compatibility. The only drawback is that the converted files may be larger than before, but that is a known downside of the AVI format as a whole.

The improvement is already live for all users. If you need a video that opens more reliably in Windows Media Player, you can [convert MP4 to AVI](https://mconverter.eu/convert/mp4/avi/) with MConverter.
