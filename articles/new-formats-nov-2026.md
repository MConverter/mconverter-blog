With the recent discontinuation of Microsoft Publisher, MConverter’s latest update focuses on offering alternatives for users who need to keep their PUB files accessible and editable.

Instead of wrestling with PowerShell scripts, you can [convert PUB to PDF](/convert/pub/pdf/) in bulk with MConverter. This way, you will only need a browser or a PDF reader to view your Publisher creations.

If you need to be sure that your PUB files are best suited for long-term archiving, you can [convert them to PDF/A](/convert/pub/pdfa.pdf/). This is a standardized version of PDF for digital preservation.

Or, if you need to edit your PUB files, you can [convert them to ODG](/convert/pub/odg/) and open them in LibreOffice Draw or other compatible applications.

Last but not least, MConverter can now also batch [export ODG to PDF](/convert/odg/pdf/), so you can share your drawings with anyone, even if they don’t have specialized software installed.

If you notice any issues with these new conversions, please [let us know](#legal) so that we can improve the conversion engine.
