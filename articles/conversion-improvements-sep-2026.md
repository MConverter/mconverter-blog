In today’s update, the focus was placed on improving the security of many types of conversions, plus fixing some annoying bugs. Notable examples include:

* [Converting multi-page draw.io diagrams to image formats like PNG](https://mconverter.eu/convert/drawio/png/) now works correctly, without duplicating the first page and skipping the last one.
* Markdown, IPYNB, HTML, and PDF conversion improvements to lists and tables, as well as a new system for math equations (MathML).
